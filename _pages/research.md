## Scalable solvers

We study domain decomposition and multilevel methods for linear and nonlinear systems arising from partial differential equations. Our interests include robust and adaptive coarse spaces, preconditioning, and algorithms that make effective use of parallel computing architectures.

## Scientific machine learning

We combine scientific computing with machine learning, bringing mathematical structure and physical knowledge into data-driven methods. Topics include neural approaches to differential equations, scalable optimisation, graph learning, and reliable surrogate models.

## Multiphysics and multiscale models

We develop discretisations, coupling algorithms, and solvers for problems involving interacting physical processes and multiple scales. Applications include cardiovascular simulation, heterogeneous materials, offshore energy, and other collaborative engineering and life-science problems.
