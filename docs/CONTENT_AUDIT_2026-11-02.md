# Content correctness audit, 2 November 2026

## Scope and standard of evidence

Reviewed the site's content inventory: 50 member/project profiles, 48 course/short-course records, 67 student-project records, 12 research-project entries, 98 publications, 168 talks, three published blog posts and two news items. Also reviewed the homepage, research themes, four software descriptions, opportunities and contact content. This is an editorial and metadata audit, not an independent scientific review of every paper, thesis or archived slide deck.

Checked all publication DOIs: 69 of 72 returned machine-readable publisher-deposited metadata. The remaining three were checked against publisher pages/search records: [IOP](https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae7495), [SIAM](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/21M1433605), and [Springer](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-14244-5_10). The [DOI evidence inventory](CONTENT_DOI_AUDIT_2026-11-02.json) records the retrieved metadata and failures. Title, author-count, year, volume, issue and page comparisons flagged records for manual investigation; registry metadata was not accepted blindly.

Member and teaching content was checked for internal consistency, source provenance and agreement with the available local CV and thesis bibliography. Public sources were checked for selected biographies, research summaries and current-project claims. This does not independently confirm every private supervision arrangement or every historical seminar date. Source records and limitations below distinguish those cases.
