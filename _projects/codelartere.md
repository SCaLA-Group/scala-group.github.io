CoDELartere investigated fluid–structure interaction in arteries, coupling blood flow with nonlinear, anisotropic arterial-wall models. The project developed monolithic solution approaches using overlapping Schwarz and Dirichlet–Neumann domain decomposition algorithms.

[DFG project results and funding period](https://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/214421492/ergebnisse)
